Welcome to RasaQuest

A delicious adventure awaits.

Explore a food-themed puzzle adventure inspired by Malaysian cuisine. Discover hidden ingredients, solve challenging puzzles, avoid traps, and unlock iconic local dishes as you progress through your journey.

Explore, collect, and unlock

Search through different puzzle areas to uncover ingredients and build your collection. Use your discoveries to unlock dishes such as Nasi Lemak, Roti Canai, Satay, and Rendang. Follow the adventure in Story Mode, or test your skills in Endless Mode with randomized boards and an ever-higher score to chase.
