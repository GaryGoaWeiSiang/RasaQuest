Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 31 October 2026  |  Last Updated: 31 October 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to RasaQuest!

RasaQuest is a food-themed puzzle adventure game developed by GaryGWS and the team. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you play RasaQuest, how it is used, how it is stored, and how you can exercise your data protection rights.

By using RasaQuest, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We May Process

Depending on the features enabled in the version of the game you use, the following information may be processed:

A. Game Progress and Local Save Data

RasaQuest stores game progress locally on your device. This may include:

  • Game progress and unlocked levels.
  • Collected ingredients and unlocked dishes.
  • Story progression.
  • Game scores and statistics.
  • Game settings and preferences.
  • Save-slot information.

This information is used to maintain your progress and allow you to continue playing.

B. Huawei Game Services

If Huawei Game Services features are enabled, the game may process information associated with your Huawei gaming account, such as:

  • Player identification information.
  • Player display name or nickname.
  • Player profile image or avatar.
  • Game achievements and leaderboard scores.

Such information is processed to support relevant game features, such as player profiles, achievements, and leaderboards.

The processing of this information may also be subject to Huawei's own privacy policies and service terms.

C. Cloud Save Data

If cloud-save functionality is enabled, selected game progress and save information may be transmitted to a cloud service to support backup and restoration.

Cloud-save information may include game progress, collected ingredients, unlocked dishes, scores, and other save-game information.

If cloud saving is not enabled in your version of RasaQuest, your game progress remains stored locally on your device.

D. Device and Technical Information

The game platform or third-party services may process limited technical information required to operate the game, troubleshoot issues, and provide platform services.

The information collected depends on the services and platform features enabled.

3. How We Use Information

Information processed by RasaQuest may be used to:

  • Save and maintain game progress.
  • Provide gameplay features and functionality.
  • Display player profile information.
  • Support achievements and leaderboard features.
  • Provide backup and restoration functionality, where enabled.
  • Diagnose technical problems and improve game stability.
  • Maintain the security and proper operation of the game.

We do not intend to use game information for purposes unrelated to providing or maintaining the game without an appropriate legal basis.

4. Data Storage and Retention

Game progress stored locally remains on the device and may be removed when the player clears the game's data, uninstalls the game, or resets the device.

If cloud storage or Huawei Game Services features are enabled, certain information may also be stored or processed by the relevant service providers according to their retention and privacy policies.

We retain information only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy, subject to applicable legal requirements.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell players' personal information.

Information may be processed by third-party service providers where necessary to provide game functionality, including:

  • Huawei and its relevant gaming platform services.
  • Cloud storage providers, if cloud-save functionality is enabled.
  • Other technical service providers that support the operation of the game, if applicable.

These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable requirements.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information associated with RasaQuest against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

7. Children's Privacy

RasaQuest does not knowingly request personal information directly from children.

Parents or legal guardians who believe that a child has provided personal information in connection with the game may contact us using the contact details below.

We will review such requests and take appropriate action in accordance with applicable laws.

8. Your Data Protection Rights

Depending on your location and applicable data protection laws, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion of personal information, where applicable.
  • Request restriction of certain processing activities.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Raise questions or complaints regarding the handling of your information.

For further information or to submit a request, please visit our Data Subject Rights page.

9. Third-Party Services

RasaQuest may rely on third-party platforms and services to provide certain functionality.

These services may have their own privacy policies and terms of use. We encourage players to review the relevant policies when using platform-specific features.

For Huawei services, please refer to Huawei's official privacy information: Huawei Privacy Policy.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when game features, services, or applicable legal requirements change.

Any updated version will be made available through the designated Privacy Policy webpage. The updated effective date will be displayed at the beginning of the policy.
