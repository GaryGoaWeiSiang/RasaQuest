Data Subject Rights

Last Updated: 31 October 2026

1. Your Privacy Rights

At RasaQuest, we respect your privacy and recognize that you may have rights concerning your personal information.

Depending on applicable data protection laws, including the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (Malaysia), you may request to exercise the following rights.

Right to Access

You may request information about the personal data we process about you and request access to that information, where applicable.

Right to Correction

You may request corrections to personal information that is inaccurate, incomplete, misleading, or outdated.

Right to Deletion

You may request deletion of personal information that we hold about you, subject to applicable legal requirements and exceptions.

Right to Withdraw Consent

Where personal information is processed based on your consent, you may withdraw that consent, subject to applicable requirements.

Right to Make a Complaint

You may contact us if you have concerns about how your personal information is collected, used, stored, or disclosed.

3. Local Game Data

RasaQuest stores certain game information locally on your device, including game progress, collected ingredients, unlocked dishes, scores, and settings.

You can remove locally stored game progress through the game's available data-clearing functionality, where provided, or by clearing the application's data through your device settings.

Please note that clearing local game data may permanently remove your saved progress.

4. Third-Party Platform Information

If you use Huawei Game Services or other third-party platform features, some information may be controlled or processed by the relevant service provider.

Requests involving information held directly by Huawei may need to be submitted to Huawei through its own privacy channels.

Huawei Privacy Policy: https://consumer.huawei.com/en/privacy/privacy-policy/.

5. Request Handling

We will review privacy requests and respond within a reasonable period in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

Some requests may be limited or declined where permitted by law, including where information must be retained to meet legal obligations.
